## Getting Started Guides

### Guide 1: Setting Up Your LUKU Account

**Time:** 5 minutes

**Steps:**
1. Open LUKU in your web browser
2. Click **"Sign Up"** on the login page
3. Enter your email address
4. Create a secure password (at least 8 characters)
5. Click **"Create Account"**
6. Check your email for a verification link (if required)
7. Click the verification link to activate your account
8. Log in with your credentials

**Tips:**
- Use a password manager for secure password storage
- Keep your email address updated for account recovery

---

### Guide 2: Building Your First Wardrobe

**Time:** 10-15 minutes

**Steps:**
1. Log in to your LUKU account
2. Navigate to **"My Wardrobe"** from the main menu
3. Click the **"Add Items"** button (plus icon)
4. Select photos of your clothing items from your device
   - You can upload multiple items at once
   - Supported formats: JPEG, PNG, WebP
5. Wait for LUKU's AI to analyze each item
   - The AI will identify category, color, style, and season
6. Review the automatically detected information
7. Edit any details if needed:
   - Click on an item to view details
   - Update name, category, color, or style
8. Repeat until you've added at least 10-15 items

**Tips:**
- Take clear, well-lit photos for better AI recognition
- Add items from different categories (tops, bottoms, dresses, etc.)
- Include items in various colors and styles
- The more items you add, the better LUKU's suggestions become

**What's Next:**
- Try generating your first outfit suggestions
- Explore the Lifestyle Hub to see your personalized dashboard

---

### Guide 3: Your First Outfit Suggestion

**Time:** 3-5 minutes

**Steps:**
1. Ensure you have at least 5-10 items in your wardrobe marked as "clean"
2. Navigate to **"Suggestions"** from the main menu
3. Select a style preference from the dropdown:
   - Start with "Casual" for everyday looks
4. (Optional) Add a custom prompt:
   - Examples: "for work", "weekend brunch", "date night"
5. Click **"Generate Outfits"**
6. Wait a few seconds for AI to create suggestions
7. Browse through the generated outfits
8. Click on any outfit to see details
9. Try the **"Try On"** button to visualize the outfit
10. Click the **heart icon** to save your favorite outfits

**Tips:**
- Start with a simple style preference, then experiment
- Use custom prompts to get occasion-specific suggestions
- Provide feedback (like/dislike) to help LUKU learn
- Save outfits you love to build your style profile

## Wardrobe Management Guides

### Guide 4: Organizing Your Wardrobe

**Time:** 10-20 minutes

**Steps:**
1. Navigate to **"My Wardrobe"**
2. Use filters to organize your view:
   - **By Category**: Tops, Bottoms, Dresses, etc.
   - **By Color**: Filter by specific colors
   - **By Style**: Casual, Formal, Sporty, etc.
   - **By Status**: Clean, In Wash, Dirty
3. Update item statuses:
   - Click on an item
   - Select the appropriate status (Clean/In Wash/Dirty)
   - This helps LUKU suggest only available items
4. Review item details:
   - Check that categories and colors are correct
   - Update any incorrect information
5. Delete items you no longer own:
   - Click on the item
   - Click the delete/trash icon
   - Confirm deletion

**Tips:**
- Keep item statuses updated for accurate suggestions
- Regularly review and clean up your wardrobe list
- Use the "Use Count" to see which items you wear most

---

### Guide 5: Managing Laundry Status

**Time:** 2-3 minutes

**Steps:**
1. Navigate to **"My Wardrobe"**
2. For individual items:
   - Click on an item
   - Change status to "In Wash" or "Dirty" as needed
3. For multiple items:
   - Update each item individually
   - Or use the bulk update feature (if available)
4. After laundry is done:
   - Go to **"Lifestyle Hub"**
   - Find the Laundry Tracker section
   - Click **"Finish Laundry"** to mark all "In Wash" items as "Clean"

**Tips:**
- Update statuses regularly to keep suggestions accurate
- Use the Laundry Tracker in Lifestyle Hub for quick updates
- Mark items as "Dirty" after wearing to track usage

## Outfit & Style Guides

### Guide 6: Creating and Saving Outfit Collections

**Time:** 5-10 minutes

**Steps:**
1. Generate outfit suggestions (see Guide 3)
2. Browse through the suggestions
3. Click the **heart icon** on outfits you like
   - These automatically go to "My Favorites" album
4. Create custom albums:
   - Navigate to **"Albums"**
   - Click **"Create Album"**
   - Enter a name (e.g., "Work Outfits", "Weekend Looks")
   - Click **"Create"**
5. Add outfits to custom albums:
   - From any outfit suggestion, click the heart icon
   - Or manually add from saved outfits
6. Organize your albums:
   - Create albums for different occasions
   - Add descriptive names
   - Remove outfits you no longer like

**Tips:**
- Create albums for different seasons or occasions
- Regularly review and update your albums
- Use albums to track your style evolution

---

### Guide 7: Discovering Your Style DNA

**Time:** 5-10 minutes

**Prerequisites:** At least 3 saved outfits in your Albums

**Steps:**
1. Save at least 3 outfits to your Albums (see Guide 6)
2. Navigate to **"Style DNA"** (or "Evolution") from the main menu
3. Click **"Analyze My Style"**
4. Wait for the AI analysis to complete
5. Review your Style DNA results:
   - **Core Style Profile**: Keywords, top colors, top categories
   - **Style Evolution Narrative**: How your style has developed
   - **Recommendations**: Personalized suggestions
6. Use the insights to:
   - Understand your style preferences
   - Discover new style directions
   - Make more informed wardrobe choices

**Tips:**
- Save more outfits over time for more accurate analysis
- Re-run the analysis periodically to see your style evolution
- Use recommendations to guide future purchases

**Time:** 5 minutes

**Steps:**
1. Navigate to **"Color Harmony"** from the main menu

**Tips:**

### Guide 9: Planning a Trip Wardrobe

**Time:** 10-15 minutes

**Steps:**
1. Navigate to **"Trip Planner"** from the main menu
2. Click **"Create New Trip"** or **"Generate Plan"**
3. Fill in trip details:
   - **Trip Name**: e.g., "Summer Vacation 2024"
   - **Destination**: e.g., "Paris, France"
   - **Start Date**: Select from calendar
   - **End Date**: Select from calendar
   - **Notes**: Add details like:
     - Weather forecast
     - Planned activities
     - Dress code requirements
     - Special events
4. Click **"Generate Plan"**
5. Wait for AI to create your trip plan
6. Review the results:
   - **Packing List**: Items to bring
   - **Daily Plans**: Outfit for each day
7. Review each day's outfit
8. (Optional) Click **"Try On"** to visualize outfits
9. (Optional) Save the trip plan as an Album for easy access

**Tips:**
- Be specific in your notes for better suggestions
- Consider the climate and activities
- Review and adjust the plan as needed
- Save the plan as an album to reference during your trip

### Guide 10: Using Virtual Try-On

**Time:** 5-10 minutes

**Steps:**
1. Navigate to **"Virtual Try-On"** from the main menu
2. Choose a try-on method:

   **Option A: From a Saved Outfit**
   - Select an outfit from your saved outfits
   - Click **"Try On"**

   **Option B: Create New Outfit**
   - Select items from your wardrobe
   - Create a new outfit combination
   - Click **"Try On"**

3. Choose your try-on mode:
   - **User Image**: Use a previously uploaded photo
   - **New Upload**: Upload a new photo of yourself
   - **Mannequin**: Use customizable mannequin settings

4. If using Mannequin:
   - Click **"Mannequin Settings"**
   - Adjust:
     - Body type
     - Height
     - Measurements (chest, waist, hips)
     - Skin tone
   - Save settings

5. Click to generate the visualization
6. Wait for AI to process the try-on
7. Review the result
8. Try different outfits or adjust settings as needed

**Tips:**
- Use clear, well-lit photos for best results
- Update mannequin settings to match your body type
- Try on multiple outfits to compare looks
- Save your favorite visualizations

## Shopping & Marketplace Guides

### Guide 11: Shopping in the Marketplace

**Time:** 5-15 minutes

**Steps:**
1. Navigate to **"Marketplace"** from the main menu
2. Browse featured products on the homepage
3. Use filters to find specific items:
   - **Search**: Enter keywords
   - **Category**: Select Top, Bottom, Dress, etc.
   - **Style**: Choose Casual, Formal, etc.
   - **Price Range**: Set min and max prices
   - Click **"Apply"** to filter
4. Click on any product to view details:
   - Product images
   - Description
   - Price
   - Available sizes
   - Seller information
   - Customer reviews
5. Select size and quantity
6. Click **"Add to Cart"**
7. Review your cart:
   - Click the cart icon in the header
   - Verify items and quantities
8. Proceed to checkout (see Guide 12)

**Tips:**
- Read product reviews before purchasing
- Check size charts if available
- Compare similar products
- Look for featured or discounted items

---

### Guide 12: Completing a Purchase

**Time:** 5-10 minutes

**Prerequisites:** Items in your cart

**Steps:**
1. Click the **cart icon** in the header
2. Review your cart:
   - Verify items and quantities
   - Check total price
   - Remove items if needed
3. Click **"Checkout"** or **"Proceed to Payment"**
4. Enter shipping information:
   - Full name
   - Street address
   - City, State, ZIP
   - Country
   - Phone number
5. Review shipping options (if available)
6. Enter payment information:
   - Card number
   - Expiration date
   - CVV
   - Billing address
7. Review order summary
8. Click **"Place Order"** or **"Complete Purchase"**
9. Wait for confirmation
10. Check your email for order confirmation

**Tips:**
- Double-check shipping address
- Save payment information for faster future checkouts (if option available)
- Keep order confirmation emails for records
- Track your order status in your account

### Guide 13: Becoming a Seller

**Time:** 15-20 minutes

**Steps:**
1. Navigate to **"More"** menu (three dots icon) and select **"Seller Dashboard"**  
   **or** open the dedicated LUKU Business portal in your browser (same domain, `/business` path)
2. Click **"Register Business Account"**
3. Fill in business information:
   - **Business Name**: Your store/brand name
   - **Business Description**: Describe what you sell
   - **Contact Email**: Business email address
   - **Logo** (optional): Upload your business logo
   - **Banner** (optional): Upload a banner image
4. Review terms and conditions
5. Submit registration

**Complete required verification:**

6. Finish **Seller KYC** (personal verification), if prompted:
   - Provide your legal name and contact information
   - Add your address and required identity details
7. Complete **Business KYC** from **Settings** once your business account exists:
   - Legal entity name and registered address
   - Registration/tax ID (where applicable)
   - Representative name and phone
8. Once verification is complete, use the **Seller Dashboard** to manage your shop

**Tips:**
- Use a professional business name
- Write a clear, compelling description
- Upload high-quality logo and banner images
- Keep your contact email active for customer inquiries

---

### Guide 14: Adding Products to Sell

**Time:** 10-15 minutes per product

**Prerequisites:** Verified seller account

**Steps:**
1. Navigate to **"Seller Dashboard"**
2. Go to **"Product Management"**
3. Click **"Add Product"** or **"New Product"**
4. Fill in product details:
   - **Name**: Product name
   - **Description**: Detailed description
   - **Price**: Set your price
   - **Category**: Select from dropdown
   - **Color**: Enter color
   - **Style**: Select style
   - **Season**: Select season
   - **Size**: Enter available sizes
   - **Stock Quantity**: How many available
5. Upload product images:
   - Click **"Upload Images"**
   - Select multiple photos (front, back, details)
   - Wait for uploads to complete
6. Set product options:
   - **Featured**: Check if you want this featured
   - **Active**: Ensure this is checked to make it visible
7. Click **"Save Product"** or **"Publish"**
8. Verify the product appears in Marketplace

**Tips:**
- Use clear, high-quality product photos
- Write detailed, accurate descriptions
- Set competitive prices
- Keep stock quantities updated
- Use featured status for your best products

**Time:** 5 minutes per order

**Prerequisites:** Seller account with orders

**Steps:**

3. View orders by status:
   - **Pending**: New orders to confirm
   - **Confirmed**: Orders ready to ship
   - **Shipped**: Orders in transit

4. For each order:

5. Update order status:
   - **Confirm Order**: Mark as confirmed
   - **Ship Order**: 
     - Add tracking number
     - Update status to "Shipped"
   - **Mark as Delivered**: When customer receives it
6. Communicate with buyers if needed
7. Handle any issues or returns

**Tips:**
- Process orders promptly
- Add tracking numbers for shipped orders
- Communicate with buyers about delays
- Keep order records for your records

**Steps:**
1. Make sure you’re signed in to your LUKU account  
2. Open the main navigation (bottom nav on mobile or the relevant menu on desktop)  
3. Go to **"My Orders"**  
4. Browse your order list:

### Guide 22: Requesting a Return or Refund

**Time:** 5-10 minutes

**Prerequisites:** An eligible order within the allowed return window

**Steps:**
1. Sign in to your LUKU account  
2. Go to **"My Orders"**  
3. Open the order that contains the item you want to return  
4. If available, click **"Request Return"** from the order view  
   - Alternatively, open the dedicated **"Returns"** section from the main navigation  
5. Select the item(s) you want to return  
6. Choose a **return reason** from the list  
7. Add **notes** to explain the issue (e.g., sizing, quality, wrong item)  
8. (Optional) Upload supporting **photos** if requested  
9. Submit your return request  
10. Check the **Returns** section to track your request status:
    - Pending → Approved/Rejected → Refunded/Completed  

**Tips:**
- Always review the seller’s or platform’s return policy before submitting a request  
- Keep any original packaging until your return is processed  
- Once a return is marked **Refunded**, it may still take a few business days to appear on your bank or card statement

### Guide 23: Using Your Wishlist

**Time:** 2-3 minutes

**Steps:**
1. While browsing the **Marketplace**, click the **heart icon** on any product to add it to your Wishlist  
2. Open your **Wishlist**:
   - Click the **heart icon** in the top header, or  
   - Navigate to the **"Wishlist"** tab from the main navigation (if available)  
3. Review your saved items:
   - Click a product to open its details page  
   - Compare items and decide what to purchase  
4. When ready to buy:
   - Open a product from your Wishlist  
   - Choose size and quantity  
   - Click **"Add to Cart"** and proceed to checkout  

**Tips:**
- Use Wishlist to keep track of items you’re considering but not ready to purchase yet  
- Wishlist does **not** reserve inventory, so items may sell out or change price over time  
- Remove items you’re no longer interested in to keep your list focused

## Troubleshooting Quick Reference

### Common Issues and Solutions

**Issue: Can't upload images**
- Check file format (JPEG, PNG, WebP)
- Ensure file isn't too large
- Check internet connection
- Try a different browser

**Issue: Outfit suggestions not generating**
- Ensure you have clean items in wardrobe
- Check internet connection
- Try refreshing the page
- Clear browser cache

**Issue: Virtual Try-On not working**
- Check internet connection
- Ensure you've selected an outfit
- Try a different try-on mode
- Update mannequin settings

**Issue: Can't save outfits**
- Ensure you're logged in
- Check internet connection
- Try refreshing the page
- Clear browser cache
