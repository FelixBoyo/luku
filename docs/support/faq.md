## Getting Started

### What is LUKU?

**LUKU** is an AI-powered wardrobe stylist that helps you discover personalized fashion recommendations, make sustainable choices, and express your unique style. Think of it as your personal style companion that learns your preferences and suggests outfits tailored just for you.

### How do I get started with LUKU?

1. **Sign up** for a free account using your email address
2. **Add items to your wardrobe** by uploading photos of your clothing
3. **Explore the Lifestyle Hub** to see your personalized dashboard
4. **Try generating outfit suggestions** to see LUKU's AI in action
5. **Save your favorite outfits** to Albums for easy access

### Is LUKU free to use?

Yes! LUKU offers a free account with access to all core features including wardrobe management, outfit suggestions, style analysis, and more. Some premium features may be available in the future.

### What devices can I use LUKU on?

LUKU is a web application that works on:
- **Desktop computers** (Windows, Mac, Linux)
- **Tablets** (iPad, Android tablets)
- **Mobile phones** (iPhone, Android)

Simply open LUKU in your web browser - no app installation required! LUKU is also available as a Progressive Web App (PWA) for an app-like experience.

## Account & Authentication

### How do I create an account?

Click the "Sign Up" button on the login page and enter your email address and a secure password. You'll receive a confirmation email to verify your account.

### What’s the difference between LUKU and LUKU Business?

- **LUKU** is your personal style companion for managing your wardrobe, generating outfits, planning trips, and shopping the marketplace as a **buyer**.  
- **LUKU Business** is the dedicated portal for **sellers** to manage their shop, products, orders, analytics, and promotions.

You can use LUKU as a buyer without ever becoming a seller. If you want to sell on LUKU, you’ll use LUKU Business in addition to your regular account.

### How do I access the LUKU Business portal?

- From your browser, go to the LUKU Business URL (the same domain as LUKU, with the `/business` path).  
- If you’re already signed in, you’ll see the business experience.  
- If you’re not signed in, you’ll see the **LUKU Business** sign-in / sign-up screen.

### I forgot my password. How do I reset it?

On the login page, click **"Forgot Password"** and enter your email address.  
You’ll receive a password reset email:

1. Open the email on your device  
2. Click the reset link  
3. You’ll be taken to LUKU’s secure reset page (the `/reset-password` route)  
4. Enter and confirm your new password

### Can I change my email address?

Currently, email changes must be done through your account settings. If you need assistance, please contact support.

### How do I sign out?

Click your profile icon in the top-right corner of the header and select "Sign Out" from the menu.

## My Wardrobe

### How do I add items to my wardrobe?

1. Navigate to **"My Wardrobe"** from the main menu
2. Click the **"Add Items"** button (usually a plus icon)
3. Upload photos of your clothing items
4. LUKU's AI will automatically analyze each item to identify:
   - Category (Top, Bottom, Dress, etc.)
   - Color
   - Style (Casual, Formal, etc.)
   - Season

### What image formats are supported?

LUKU supports common image formats including:
- JPEG/JPG
- PNG
- WebP

### How many items can I add to my wardrobe?

There's no hard limit on the number of items you can add to your wardrobe. Add as many items as you'd like to get better outfit suggestions!

### Can I edit item details after uploading?

Yes! Click on any item in your wardrobe to view its details. You can update the name, category, color, style, and other attributes.

### What are item statuses (Clean, In Wash, Dirty)?

Item statuses help you track which clothes are ready to wear:
- **Clean**: Item is clean and ready to wear
- **In Wash**: Item is currently being laundered
- **Dirty**: Item needs to be washed

You can update an item's status by clicking on it and selecting the appropriate status. This helps LUKU suggest outfits using only items that are available to wear.

### How do I delete an item from my wardrobe?

1. Click on the item you want to delete
2. Look for the delete/trash icon
3. Confirm the deletion

**Note:** If an item is used in saved outfits, you'll be notified. The item will be removed from those outfits, but the outfits themselves will remain.

### What is "Use Count"?

Use Count shows how many times an item appears in your saved outfits. This helps you see which items you wear most often and identify pieces that might need more styling attention.

### Can I organize my wardrobe into categories?

LUKU automatically categorizes items when you upload them. You can filter and view items by category, color, style, or season using the wardrobe filters.

## Outfit Suggestions & Discovery

### How do I get outfit suggestions?

1. Navigate to **"Suggestions"** or **"Discover"** from the main menu
2. Select a style preference (Casual, Formal, Sporty, etc.)
3. Optionally add a custom prompt (e.g., "for a date night" or "work-appropriate")
4. Click **"Generate Outfits"**

LUKU will create multiple outfit combinations using items from your wardrobe.

### What style preferences are available?

LUKU supports various style preferences including:
- Casual
- Formal
- Sporty
- Vintage
- Bohemian
- Minimalist
- Streetwear
- Classic

### Can I customize outfit suggestions?

Yes! You can:
- Select a specific style preference
- Add custom prompts to describe the occasion or vibe you want
- Provide feedback on suggestions (like/dislike) to help LUKU learn your preferences

### How does LUKU learn my style preferences?

LUKU learns from:
- Items you add to your wardrobe
- Outfits you save to Albums
- Feedback you provide (likes/dislikes)
- Your Style DNA analysis

The more you use LUKU, the better it understands your personal style!

### What's the difference between "Suggestions" and "Discover"?

- **Suggestions**: Focused outfit generation based on your wardrobe and style preferences
- **Discover**: A comprehensive discovery hub that combines Suggestions, Fashion Trends, and Style DNA analysis in one place

### Can I see outfit suggestions for specific occasions?

Yes! Use the custom prompt field when generating outfits. For example:
- "for a job interview"
- "weekend brunch"
- "date night"
- "work meeting"
- "gym workout"

## Style DNA & Analysis

### What is Style DNA?

Style DNA is LUKU's analysis of your personal fashion evolution. It identifies:
- Your core style profile (keywords, colors, categories)
- How your style has evolved
- Personalized recommendations for your wardrobe

### How do I get my Style DNA analysis?

1. Navigate to **"Style DNA"** (or "Evolution") from the main menu
2. Make sure you have at least **3 saved outfits** in your Albums
3. Click **"Analyze My Style"**

### Why do I need at least 3 saved outfits?

Style DNA analysis requires multiple saved outfits to identify patterns in your preferences. Save your favorite outfit suggestions to Albums first!

### What information does Style DNA provide?

Style DNA shows:
- **Core Style Profile**: Keywords, top colors, and top categories that define your style
- **Style Evolution Narrative**: A description of how your style has developed
- **Personalized Recommendations**: Suggestions for items or styles to explore

## Color Harmony

### What is Color Harmony?

Color Harmony helps you discover perfect color combinations for any item in your wardrobe using color theory principles. It suggests complementary, analogous, or triadic color schemes.

### How do I use Color Harmony?

1. Navigate to **"Color Harmony"** from the main menu
2. Select an item from your wardrobe
3. Click **"Analyze Colors"**
4. View color theory explanations and suggested outfit combinations

### What color theory principles does LUKU use?

LUKU uses professional color theory including:
- **Complementary colors**: Colors opposite on the color wheel
- **Analogous colors**: Colors next to each other on the color wheel
- **Triadic colors**: Three evenly spaced colors on the color wheel

### Can I save Color Harmony suggestions?

Yes! You can favorite any outfit suggestion from Color Harmony, and it will be saved to your "My Favorites" album.

## Virtual Try-On

### What is Virtual Try-On?

Virtual Try-On uses AI to visualize how outfits look on you. You can see outfit combinations before wearing them.

### How do I use Virtual Try-On?

1. Navigate to **"Virtual Try-On"** from the main menu
2. Select an outfit from your saved outfits or create a new one
3. Choose a try-on mode:
   - **User Image**: Use a photo you've uploaded
   - **New Upload**: Upload a new photo
   - **Mannequin**: Use a customizable mannequin
4. Click to generate the visualization

### What are Mannequin Settings?

Mannequin Settings let you customize a virtual mannequin to match your body type, height, measurements, and skin tone for more accurate try-on visualizations.

### How do I update my Mannequin Settings?

1. In Virtual Try-On, click on Mannequin Settings
2. Adjust:
   - Body type
   - Height
   - Measurements (chest, waist, hips)
   - Skin tone
3. Save your settings

### Can I try on outfits from Suggestions or Trends?

Yes! Click the "Try On" button on any outfit card in Suggestions, Trends, or Discover to automatically open Virtual Try-On with that outfit.

## Marketplace & Shopping

### What is the Marketplace?

The Marketplace is LUKU's shopping section where you can browse and purchase fashion items from verified sellers.

### How do I browse products?

1. Navigate to **"Marketplace"** from the main menu
2. Browse featured products or use filters to search:
   - Category
   - Color
   - Style
   - Price range
   - Search terms

### How do I view product details?

Click on any product card to see:
- Product images
- Description
- Price
- Size and availability
- Seller information
- Reviews

### How do I add items to my cart?

1. View a product's details
2. Select size and quantity
3. Click **"Add to Cart"**

### How do I checkout?

1. Click the cart icon in the header
2. Review your items
3. Enter shipping information
4. Complete payment through our secure checkout

### What payment methods are accepted?

LUKU uses Stripe for secure payments. Accepted methods include major credit cards and other payment options supported by Stripe.

### How do I use my Wishlist?

- Click the **heart icon** on any product card in the Marketplace to add it to your Wishlist.  
- Open your **Wishlist** from:
  - The heart icon in the top header, or  
  - The **Wishlist** tab in the main navigation (on some devices).  
- From your Wishlist you can:
  - View all saved items in one place  
  - Click a product to see details  
  - Move items from Wishlist to Cart when you’re ready to buy  

Wishlist items are not reserved; availability and price can change until you complete a purchase.

### How do I view my orders?

You can see all of your past and current orders in the **My Orders** section:

1. Open the main navigation (bottom nav on mobile or the relevant menu on desktop)  
2. Go to **My Orders**  
3. Browse orders by their status and date  

Each order shows:
- Products purchased  
- Total amount  
- Shipping details  
- Current status and (if available) tracking information

### What do order statuses mean?

LUKU uses the following order statuses:

- **Pending** – Your order was placed and is awaiting confirmation from the seller  
- **Confirmed** – The seller has confirmed your order and is preparing it  
- **Shipped** – The seller has handed your order to a carrier; it’s on its way  
- **Delivered** – Your order has been delivered to the shipping address  
- **Cancelled** – The order has been cancelled (by you, the seller, or support)

### How do returns and refunds work?

If you’re not satisfied with a purchase and it’s within the return window:

1. Go to **Returns** from the main navigation (or via **My Orders → Request Return** if available)  
2. Select the order or item you want to return  
3. Choose a **reason** for the return and add any **notes** or **photos** (if requested)  
4. Submit your return request  

Your return will move through these statuses:

- **Pending** – Return request submitted and under review  
- **Approved** – Your return was accepted; follow the instructions provided (e.g., shipping the item back)  
- **Rejected** – The return did not meet the policy requirements  
- **Refunded** – Your refund has been issued via the original payment method  
- **Completed** – The return process is fully closed

Refund timing can vary depending on your bank or payment provider, but most refunds appear within a few business days after they are marked **Refunded**.

### Where can I see the status of my returns?

- Open the **Returns** section from the main navigation to see all your return requests and their statuses.  
- You can also open an individual order from **My Orders** to see if any of its items are part of a return.

### Can I leave product reviews?

Yes! After purchasing a product, you can leave a review with a rating and written feedback to help other shoppers.

## Seller Features

### How do I become a seller on LUKU?

1. Navigate to **"More"** menu
2. Select **"Seller Dashboard"**
3. Click **"Register Business Account"**
4. Complete the registration form with:
   - Business name
   - Business description
   - Contact email
   - Logo and banner (optional)
5. Submit for verification

**Note:** You can also open the dedicated LUKU Business portal directly in your browser (same domain, `/business` path) to access the seller experience.

### What can I do in the Seller Dashboard?

- **Product Management**: Add, edit, and manage your products
- **Order Dashboard**: View and manage customer orders
- **Analytics**: Track sales and performance
- **Promotions**: Create discounts and special offers
- **Reviews**: Respond to customer reviews

### How do I add products to sell?

1. Go to **Seller Dashboard** → **Product Management**
2. Click **"Add Product"**
3. Fill in product details:
   - Name and description
   - Price
   - Category, color, style, season
   - Size and stock quantity
   - Upload product images
4. Save the product

### How do I manage orders?

1. Go to **Seller Dashboard** → **Order Dashboard**
2. View orders by status (Pending, Confirmed, Shipped, Delivered)
3. Update order status and add tracking numbers
4. Communicate with buyers as needed

### Can I create promotions?

Yes! Use the **Promotion Manager** in your Seller Dashboard to create:
- Percentage discounts
- Fixed amount discounts
- Bundle deals
- Flash sales

### What is KYC and why is it required for sellers?

**KYC (Know Your Customer)** is a standard identity verification process required to keep buyers and sellers safe and to comply with payment and financial regulations.  

For LUKU sellers, KYC helps:
- Confirm that you are a real person or registered business  
- Reduce fraud and abusive behavior  
- Enable secure payouts through payment providers (such as Stripe)

### What’s the difference between Seller KYC and Business KYC?

LUKU separates verification into two layers:

- **Seller KYC (Personal)** – Verifies you as an individual seller  
  - Basic personal details (name, contact information, address)  
- **Business KYC** – Verifies your **business entity** once you create a business account  
  - Legal entity name  
  - Registered business address  
  - Registration/tax ID (where applicable)  
  - Business representative contact details  

You’ll typically:
1. Complete **Seller KYC** first (personal identity verification)  
2. Then create a **Business Account**  
3. Then complete **Business KYC** from the **Settings** section of the Seller Dashboard

### How do I know if my business is verified?

- In the **Seller Dashboard**, you may see prompts or banners asking you to complete verification if anything is missing.  
- Once verification is complete, these prompts disappear and your business will show as **verified**.  
- In some cases, you’ll also receive an email confirming that your business verification is complete.

## Technical Issues

### The app is loading slowly. What should I do?

- Check your internet connection
- Try refreshing the page
- Clear your browser cache
- Try using a different browser
- Close other browser tabs to free up memory

### Images aren't uploading. What's wrong?

- Check your internet connection
- Ensure the image file isn't too large (try resizing)
- Verify the image format is supported (JPEG, PNG, WebP)
- Try a different browser
- Check if you have sufficient storage space

### Outfit suggestions aren't generating. Why?

- Ensure you have at least a few items in your wardrobe marked as "clean"
- Check your internet connection
- Try refreshing the page
- If the issue persists, contact support

### I'm seeing an error message. What should I do?

- Note the exact error message
- Try refreshing the page
- Check if you're logged in
- Clear your browser cache
- If the error persists, contact support with the error details

### The app looks different on my phone. Is this normal?

Yes! LUKU is responsive and adapts to different screen sizes. On mobile devices, you'll see a bottom navigation bar for easier access to main features.

### Can I use LUKU offline?

LUKU requires an internet connection to function, as it uses AI services and cloud storage. However, you can view previously loaded content while offline.

## Privacy & Data

### Is my data secure?

Yes! LUKU uses industry-standard security measures to protect your data. We use Supabase for secure authentication and data storage.

### What data does LUKU collect?

LUKU collects:
- Account information (email, password)
- Wardrobe items and images you upload
- Saved outfits and preferences
- Usage data to improve the service

### Can I delete my account and data?

Yes. To delete your account, please contact support. We'll remove all your data in accordance with our privacy policy.

### Who can see my wardrobe and outfits?

Your wardrobe and outfits are private to your account. Only you can see your personal style data unless you choose to share it.

### Does LUKU share my data with third parties?

LUKU does not sell your personal data. We may share anonymized, aggregated data for analytics purposes. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

### How does LUKU use AI?

LUKU uses Google Gemini AI to:
- Analyze clothing items from images
- Generate outfit suggestions
- Provide style analysis
- Create fashion trend insights

Your images and data are processed securely and are not used to train AI models without your consent.
