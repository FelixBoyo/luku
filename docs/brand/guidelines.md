# LUKU Brand Guidelines

> **Superseded for mobile/landing alignment:** Use [`docs/qa/LANDING_RN_BRAND_ALIGNMENT.md`](../qa/LANDING_RN_BRAND_ALIGNMENT.md) as the canonical brand spec. The RN app (`apps/mobile/src/theme/tokens.ts`) is source of truth. This document describes legacy web branding (indigo/purple) and is retained for historical reference only.

## Brand Name
**LUKU** (always uppercase)

## Tagline
*Your Personal Style Companion*

## Mission Statement
LUKU empowers individuals to express their unique style through AI-powered fashion recommendations while promoting sustainable choices and conscious living.

---

## Brand Personality

- **Empowering** - We help users discover and own their personal style
- **Inclusive** - Fashion for everyone, every body, every budget
- **Sustainable** - Conscious choices for a better planet
- **Innovative** - Cutting-edge AI meets timeless style wisdom
- **Approachable** - Professional yet friendly, never intimidating

---

## Visual Identity

### Logo
- Primary logo uses gradient from indigo (#6366f1) to purple (#a855f7)
- Sparkle icon represents AI-powered insights and transformation
- Always maintain clear space around logo (minimum 20px)
- Logo should never be stretched or distorted

### Color Palette

**Primary Colors:**
- **Indigo:** `#6366f1` - Intelligence, trust, professionalism
- **Purple:** `#a855f7` - Creativity, luxury, innovation
- **Pink Accent:** `#ec4899` - Energy, passion, modernity

**Secondary Colors:**
- **Green:** `#10b981` - Sustainability, growth
- **Blue:** `#3b82f6` - Wellness, calm
- **Yellow:** `#f59e0b` - Optimism, warmth

**Neutrals:**
- Gray 50-900 scale for backgrounds and typography
- White for clean, modern aesthetics

### Typography
- **Headings:** System font stack, bold weight, tight tracking
- **Body:** Sans-serif, regular weight, readable line height
- **Accent:** Light weight for taglines and subtitles

### Iconography
- Line-based icons (1.5px-2.5px stroke weight)
- Rounded corners (strokeLinecap="round")
- Consistent sizing:
  - Button icons: w-5 h-5
  - Section headers: w-8 h-8
  - Hero icons: w-16 h-16

## Voice & Tone

### Do:
- ✅ Use encouraging, positive language
- ✅ Celebrate individual expression
- ✅ Focus on sustainability without guilt-tripping
- ✅ Be conversational and friendly
- ✅ Use inclusive language (all bodies, all budgets, all styles)
- ✅ Empower users to make their own choices

### Don't:
- ❌ Use judgmental or restrictive language
- ❌ Focus on "fixing" or "problems"
- ❌ Make assumptions about body types or preferences
- ❌ Promote fast fashion or wasteful consumption
- ❌ Use overly technical jargon
- ❌ Be condescending or patronizing

### Example Copy

**Good:**
- "Discover outfits that celebrate your unique style"
- "Build a sustainable wardrobe that works for you"
- "Your style evolution starts here"
- "Express yourself with confidence"

**Avoid:**
- "Fix your wardrobe mistakes"
- "Hide problem areas"
- "Must-have items for your body type"
- "Look younger/thinner/better"

1. **Personalization** - AI learns your style and preferences
2. **Sustainability** - Make eco-conscious fashion choices
3. **Empowerment** - Express yourself with confidence
4. **Versatility** - Maximize your existing wardrobe

- Environmentally aware consumers

## Brand Applications

### Social Media
- Use gradient backgrounds for posts
- Include LUKU logo in corner
- Maintain 60/40 split: content/white space
- Use hashtags: #LUKU #SustainableStyle #YourStyleDNA

### Email Communications
- Subject lines should be empowering and actionable
- Use first-person language to build connection
- Always include sustainability tip or affirmation

### User Interface
- Consistent use of gradient for primary CTAs
- Generous white space
- Card-based layouts with soft shadows
- Smooth transitions and animations

---

## Content Guidelines

### User-Facing Messages

**Welcome Messages:**
- "Welcome to LUKU"
- "Your Personal Style Companion"
- "Discover personalized fashion, sustainable styling, and lifestyle empowerment"

**Empty States:**
- "Add items to unlock personalized insights"
- "Get started by uploading your first wardrobe item"
- "Use LUKU's outfit suggestions to find looks you love"

**Success Messages:**
- "Outfit added to your favorites!"
- "Your style is evolving beautifully"
- "Great choice for sustainable fashion!"

**Error Messages:**
- Keep them helpful and solution-oriented
- Never blame the user
- Provide clear next steps

## Compliance & Legal

### Disclaimers
- AI suggestions are recommendations, not guarantees
- User data privacy is paramount
- Sustainability metrics are estimates

### Accessibility
- Maintain WCAG 2.1 AA compliance
- Ensure color contrast ratios meet standards
- Provide alt text for all images
- Support keyboard navigation
