## 🎨 Brand Identity Summary

### Brand Name

**LUKU** (always uppercase)

### Tagline

_Your Personal Style Companion_

### Mission

Empower individuals to express their unique style through AI-powered fashion recommendations while promoting sustainable choices and conscious living.

### Core Values

1. **Empowerment** - Help users discover and own their personal style
2. **Inclusivity** - Fashion for everyone, every body, every budget
3. **Sustainability** - Conscious choices for a better planet
4. **Innovation** - Cutting-edge AI meets timeless style wisdom
5. **Approachability** - Professional yet friendly, never intimidating

### Visual Identity

-   **Primary Colors:** Indigo (#6366f1) → Purple (#a855f7) gradient
-   **Accent:** Pink (#ec4899)
-   **Logo:** L-shaped design with sparkle accent
-   **Typography:** System fonts, bold headings, clean body text

### Voice & Tone

-   Encouraging and positive
-   Celebrates individual expression
-   Sustainability-focused without judgment
-   Conversational and friendly
-   Inclusive language
