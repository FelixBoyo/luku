# LUKU Messaging Matrix

**Last Updated:** February 2026  
**Status:** v1 – aligned with `MARKETING_STRATEGY.md`, `FEATURE_HIGHLIGHTS.md`, and `MULTI_SIDED_PERSONAS.md`

---

## Core Narrative (Single Story)

> **LUKU is your personal style companion – a place to create DIY mood boards, get AI-driven outfit and style recommendations, and connect inspiration to real products you can actually wear or buy.**

All channel and persona-specific messaging should be a **variation of this core story**, not a separate story.

- **Hero capabilities:** DIY Mood Boards + AI-Driven Recommendations  
- **Supporting ideas:** Sustainability, confidence, creativity, smart shopping, real-world outfits

---

## Matrix by Persona & Funnel Stage

Personas here reference `MULTI_SIDED_PERSONAS.md`:

- **Consumer** – Style-Seeking Consumer  
- **Pro** – Creators & Style Professionals  
- **Seller** – Brands & Retailers / Sellers

Funnel stages:

- **Awareness** – first touch, curiosity
- **Consideration** – evaluating whether LUKU is “for them”
- **Activation** – taking first key actions (signup, first board, first AI use)
- **Retention / Expansion** – ongoing use, upsell, deeper features

---

### 1. Consumer (Style-Seeking User)

**Primary job:** “Help me see, plan, and evolve my style easily.”

#### Awareness

- **Core message:**  
  - “See your style come to life – create beautiful mood boards and get AI outfit ideas in minutes.”
- **Channel examples:**
  - **Paid Social:** “Turn screenshots and saved posts into real outfits with AI.”  
  - **Organic Social:** “From inspiration folder to outfits you actually wear.”  
  - **Site hero:** “Create your first mood board and let AI build outfits from your wardrobe.”

#### Consideration

- **Core message:**  
  - “LUKU turns your existing wardrobe into new outfit possibilities, not just another shopping app.”
- **Key proof points:**
  - AI learns from your actual items and feedback.
  - Mood boards organize outfits by event, season, or vibe.
  - Sustainability and smart use of what you already own.

- **Channel examples:**
  - **Website sections:** “How it works” – wardrobe → boards → AI → smarter shopping.
  - **Email:** “3 ways LUKU saves you time every morning.”

#### Activation

- **Core message:**  
  - “In your first session, you’ll add a few items, create a simple board, and get AI to plan your next looks.”
- **CTA framing:**
  - “Add 5 items and we’ll build your first outfits.”
  - “Create a ‘Next Week’ board and let AI fill it.”

- **Channel examples:**
  - **In-app copy (Auth/empty states):** “Create your first mood board – your outfits, beautifully organized.”  
  - **Onboarding email:** “Tonight, create your first board in under 5 minutes.”

#### Retention / Expansion

- **Core message:**  
  - “LUKU grows with your style – new boards, smarter AI, and inspiration for every season and event.”
- **Channel examples:**
  - **Lifecycle email:** “New season, new board – refresh your style for spring.”  
  - **In-app nudges:** “Add this outfit to a board to plan your week.”

### 2. Creators & Style Professionals

**Primary job:** “Help me deliver and present polished style concepts fast.”

#### Awareness

- **Core message:**  
  - “Client-ready mood boards and AI-assisted style concepts in one place.”
- **Channel examples:**
  - **Paid Social (creator targeting):** “Stop juggling slides and screenshots – present style boards your clients actually get.”  
  - **Landing section:** “For stylists and creators – professional boards powered by AI.”

#### Consideration

- **Core message:**  
  - “Use LUKU as your creative studio – build boards per client, explore ideas with AI, and turn them into shoppable looks.”
- **Key proof points:**
  - Board-per-client workflows.
  - AI to quickly generate variations for the same brief.
  - Potential to share boards as deliverables or portfolio.

- **Channel examples:**
  - **Site page:** “LUKU for Stylists & Creators.”  
  - **Email:** “How LUKU helps you present three strong options in half the time.”

#### Activation

- **Core message:**  
  - “Create your first ‘Client’ board, drop in looks, and use AI to explore alternatives – all in your first session.”
- **Channel examples:**
  - **In-app education:** Empty state copy in Albums: “Try a board named ‘Client – Q2 Campaign’ and drop in your looks.”  
  - **Email:** “Template: your first client board in LUKU.”

#### Retention / Expansion

- **Core message:**  
  - “Turn LUKU into your ongoing portfolio and idea lab – boards across clients, seasons, and campaigns.”
- **Channel examples:**
  - **Email:** “3 ways pros are using LUKU for client work.”  
  - **Case studies:** “How [Stylist] uses LUKU boards to close projects faster.”

### 3. Brands & Retailers / Sellers

**Primary job:** “Help me reach high-intent buyers and show my products in styled contexts.”

#### Awareness

- **Core message:**  
  - “Sell where buyers are in ‘style & shop’ mode, not just scrolling.”
- **Channel examples:**
  - **Seller landing hero:** “Stop chasing likes. Start getting orders.”  
  - **Ads:** “From IG DMs to real checkout – sell on LUKU.”

#### Consideration

- **Core message:**  
  - “LUKU gives you a real shop – discovery, AI recommendations, mood boards, and secure checkout in one place.”
- **Key proof points:**
  - Always-on catalog (not feed-based).
  - AI & boards show products inside complete outfits.
  - Trust (payments, reviews, verification).

- **Channel examples:**
  - **Explainer section:** “Why sellers move from IG to LUKU” (repurposing `SELLER_ACQUISITION_BRAINSTORM.md`).  
  - **Email:** “5 reasons fashion sellers are moving to LUKU.”

#### Activation

- **Core message:**  
  - “List your first 5 products, get them into looks, and see how buyers actually use them.”
- **Channel examples:**
  - **Onboarding email:** “Add your first 5 items – we’ll help surface them in AI outfits.”  
  - **In-dashboard tips:** “Create a ‘New Arrivals’ mood board and link it in your socials.”

#### Retention / Expansion

- **Core message:**  
  - “Use analytics, boards, and AI insights to grow repeat purchases and optimize your catalog.”
- **Channel examples:**
  - **Seller newsletter:** “What your LUKU data says about your top looks.”  
  - **In-app prompts:** “This product performs well in AI outfits – consider featuring it in a campaign.”

## How to Use This Matrix

- **When writing copy**, start from the **core narrative**, pick the **persona** and **funnel stage**, then adapt the closest example line to your channel.
- **When designing campaigns**, ensure that:
  - Each campaign clearly states its **primary persona** and **primary funnel stage**.
  - The benefit framing is consistent with the matrix (no off-brand promises).
- **When updating product surfaces** (Auth screen, empty states, tooltips), reuse the phrasing patterns from the relevant persona’s **Activation** messages to keep in-product copy aligned with marketing.
