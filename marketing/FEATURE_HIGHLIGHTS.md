## 🎨 DIY Mood Boards (Albums Feature)

### Short Description (50 words)
Create beautiful, custom collections of your favorite outfits with LUKU's DIY Mood Boards. Organize your style by occasion, season, or theme. Express your unique fashion story through curated visual collections that inspire and help you plan your looks.

### Medium Description (100 words)
LUKU's DIY Mood Boards empower users to express their personal style through custom collections. Whether organizing outfits for "Summer Vacation," "Work Essentials," or "Date Night," users can create unlimited mood boards to capture their fashion vision. Each mood board displays outfit previews in an elegant collage format, making it easy to visualize and plan looks. Users can save favorite outfits from AI recommendations, manually curated combinations, or trip plans directly into their mood boards. This feature transforms outfit organization from a chore into a creative, inspiring experience that celebrates individual style expression.

### Long Description (200 words)
LUKU's DIY Mood Boards feature revolutionizes how users organize and express their personal style. This powerful tool allows fashion enthusiasts to create unlimited custom collections, transforming outfit planning into an inspiring, visual experience.

**Key Capabilities:**
Users can create mood boards for any purpose - seasonal collections, special events, work attire, vacation planning, or simply organizing favorite looks. Each mood board displays outfit previews in an elegant, Instagram-style collage, making it easy to visualize complete collections at a glance.

**Seamless Integration:**
Mood boards integrate seamlessly with LUKU's other features. Users can save AI-recommended outfits, manually curated combinations, trip planner suggestions, or color harmony results directly into their collections. This creates a centralized hub for all style inspiration.

**Creative Expression:**
Beyond organization, mood boards serve as a creative outlet. Users can experiment with different themes, mix and match styles, and build visual narratives of their fashion journey. The feature encourages users to think intentionally about their style and discover new outfit combinations.

**User Benefits:**
- Visual inspiration at your fingertips
- Plan outfits for special events
- Track style evolution over time
- Share style stories with friends
- Reduce decision fatigue through pre-planned looks

Mood Boards transform LUKU from a utility into a creative platform where users can celebrate and express their unique fashion identity.

## 🤖 AI-Driven Recommendations

### Short Description (50 words)
LUKU's AI learns your style preferences and suggests personalized outfit combinations from your existing wardrobe. The more you use it, the smarter it gets. Save time, discover new looks, and build confidence with AI-powered style recommendations tailored just for you.

### Medium Description (100 words)
LUKU's AI-Driven Recommendations feature acts as your personal stylist, analyzing your wardrobe and learning your preferences to suggest outfits you'll actually love. Using advanced machine learning, the AI considers your clothing items, style preferences, color choices, and feedback to generate personalized suggestions.

**How It Works:**
The AI analyzes your wardrobe items, including categories, colors, styles, and combinations. It learns from your interactions - when you give thumbs up or down on suggestions, it adapts to better understand your taste. Over time, recommendations become increasingly accurate and aligned with your personal style.

**Key Benefits:**
- Personalized suggestions based on your wardrobe
- Learns and adapts to your preferences
- Saves time on daily outfit decisions
- Discovers new combinations you might not have considered
- Reduces decision fatigue

Users can customize suggestions by selecting a style vibe (Casual, Formal, Streetwear, etc.) or adding custom prompts for specific occasions or preferences. The AI then generates multiple outfit combinations, each with a creative name and item breakdown.

### Long Description (250 words)
LUKU's AI-Driven Recommendations represent the cutting edge of personalized fashion technology, combining advanced artificial intelligence with deep understanding of style preferences to deliver outfit suggestions that feel personally curated.

**Intelligent Learning System:**
At the heart of this feature is a sophisticated machine learning algorithm that continuously evolves. When you first use LUKU, the AI analyzes your wardrobe items - their categories, colors, styles, and characteristics. As you interact with suggestions, providing feedback through thumbs up or down buttons, the system learns your preferences, style boundaries, and aesthetic inclinations.

**Personalization at Scale:**
The AI doesn't just match items randomly. It understands:
- Your preferred color combinations
- Style aesthetics you gravitate toward
- Occasion-appropriate dressing
- Items you frequently combine
- Styles you tend to avoid

**Customization Options:**
Users have full control over their recommendations:
- **Style Selection:** Choose from Casual, Formal, Streetwear, Bohemian, Minimalist, or Avant-Garde
- **Custom Prompts:** Add specific instructions like "for a rainy day," "neutral colors only," or "inspired by 90s fashion"
- **Feedback Loop:** Thumbs up/down on each suggestion helps the AI learn
- **Iterative Improvement:** Each interaction makes future suggestions more accurate

**Real-World Impact:**
Users report saving 20-30 minutes daily on outfit decisions, discovering new combinations from existing wardrobe items, and building confidence in their style choices. The feature particularly benefits busy professionals, style explorers, and those looking to maximize their wardrobe's potential.

**Technical Excellence:**
Powered by Google Gemini 2.5 Flash, LUKU's AI recommendations leverage state-of-the-art natural language processing and image analysis to understand both textual descriptions and visual characteristics of clothing items, ensuring suggestions are both stylistically sound and practically wearable.

## 🎨 Color Harmony

### Short Description (50 words)
Discover perfect color combinations using color theory principles. Upload any clothing item and LUKU analyzes it to suggest complementary, analogous, or triadic color schemes. Build cohesive, visually appealing outfits with confidence.

### Medium Description (100 words)
Color Harmony uses color theory to help users create visually appealing outfits. By analyzing any clothing item's color, the feature suggests complementary colors, analogous palettes, or triadic combinations based on established color theory principles.

**How It Works:**
Users upload or select a clothing item, and LUKU's AI analyzes its color characteristics. The system then suggests:
- Complementary colors (opposites on color wheel)
- Analogous colors (adjacent on color wheel)
- Triadic combinations (three evenly spaced colors)
- Monochromatic variations

**Benefits:**
- Create visually cohesive outfits
- Learn color theory principles
- Discover new color combinations
- Build confidence in color choices
- Enhance overall style sophistication
