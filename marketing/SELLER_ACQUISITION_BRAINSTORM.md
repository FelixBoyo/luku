# Moving Sellers from Social Media to LUKU
## Brainstorm: Pain Points → Platform Value

**Purpose:** Turn Instagram/social sellers into LUKU sellers by speaking to their real frustrations and positioning LUKU as the solution.

---

## Pain point → LUKU angle (summary)

| # | Their problem | LUKU’s answer in one line |
|---|----------------|---------------------------|
| 1 | Finding right customers & converting views to sales | **Intent-based buyers** – people on LUKU are in “shop” mode, not scroll mode. |
| 2 | Consistency of posting | **One catalog, always live** – list once, products stay discoverable; no feed algorithm. |
| 3 | Buyers trust IG pages less than platforms | **Platform trust** – LUKU brand, verified sellers, reviews, secure checkout. |
| 4 | Unpredictable reach | **Stable discovery** – search, filters, recommendations; your items don’t “expire” in a feed. |

## 1. Finding the right customers & converting views into sales

**Their words:** *“Finding the right customers and converting views into actual sales is the biggest challenge. A platform that solves that would make sense for me.”*

**LUKU angle:**
- On social, intent is mixed (entertainment, inspiration, shopping). On LUKU, **marketplace + wardrobe/shopping features** = users are already in a “style + shop” mindset.
- **AI recommendations** can surface your products when someone is building an outfit or browsing by style/color – i.e. high-intent moments.

**Tactics to move sellers:**
- **Messaging:** “Your products reach people who are already looking to buy – not just scroll.”
- **Seller story:** “On IG I got likes; on LUKU I get add-to-cart.”
- **Product:** Ensure marketplace and outfit recommendations clearly tie to “Shop this look” / “Complete the look” so views are qualified.
- **Metrics to show sellers:** Conversion rate (views → add to cart / purchase) on LUKU vs “engagement” on social; emphasize intent.

---

## 2. Consistency of posting

**Their words:** *“Consistency of posting items.”*

**LUKU angle:**
- **Catalog, not feed.** List once; products stay live and searchable. No “post or disappear” pressure.
- No algorithm deciding who sees today’s post; your full catalog is available to anyone browsing or searching.

**Tactics to move sellers:**
- **Messaging:** “Your shop is always open. List once, sell 24/7 – no daily posts required.”
- **Onboarding:** “Bulk import” or “Add your first 5 items in under 10 minutes” to lower the consistency burden from day one.
- **Seller tip:** “Use IG for vibes and new drops; use LUKU for your full catalog and actual sales.”

---

## 3. Buyers trust established platforms more than individual IG pages

**Their words:** *“Buyers trust established platforms more than individual IG pages, hii sana sana.”*

**LUKU angle:**
- **Platform trust:** Checkout and brand are LUKU, not “send money to this number.”
- **Seller verification badge** and **reviews/ratings** build trust without needing a huge following.
- **Secure payments** (Stripe) and clear policies (refunds, etc.) signal “real” marketplace.

**Tactics to move sellers:**
- **Messaging:** “Sell under a name buyers already trust – LUKU. Get a verified badge and reviews, not just DMs.”
- **Trust page:** Simple “Buy safely on LUKU” (payments, protection, verified sellers, reviews).
- **Seller story:** “Buyers stopped asking ‘is this legit?’ when I moved my catalog to LUKU.”

---

## 4. Instagram reach is unpredictable

**Their words:** *“Instagram reach is unpredictable; you can post and still not reach buyers.”*

**LUKU angle:**
- **Discovery that doesn’t depend on one post:** Search, category, style/color filters, and AI “complete the look” recommendations.
- Products are found by **what they are**, not by how well a post performed today.

**Tactics to move sellers:**
- **Messaging:** “Your items are found by people searching for what you sell – not by whether the algorithm liked your post.”
- **Seller education:** “On LUKU, good titles, categories, and tags drive discovery; you’re not fighting a feed.”
- **Product:** Strong filters and search (category, style, color, price) so buyers can find sellers like you.

---

## 5. Too much time on content instead of actual selling

**Their words:** *“A lot of time goes into content instead of actual selling.”*

**LUKU angle:**
- **Product-first:** Listing is about photo, price, description, category – not Reels or stories.
- Optional: link to mood boards / style content for sellers who want it, but **selling doesn’t require being a content creator**.

**Tactics to move sellers:**
- **Messaging:** “Spend time on your products and orders, not on editing Reels. List in minutes, not hours.”
- **Onboarding:** “No content calendar – just add items and we handle discovery.”
- **Seller story:** “I stopped making 5 Reels a week and still sell more on LUKU.”

---

## 6. Window shoppers who browse but don’t buy

**Their words:** *“IG has many window shoppers; they browse but they don’t buy. A platform that attracts buyers ready to purchase would do.”*

**LUKU angle:**
- **Intent:** People open the marketplace or “Shop” to buy or seriously consider buying.
- **Friction:** One-click add to cart, saved carts, checkout in-app – fewer drop-offs than “DM to order.”

**Tactics to move sellers:**
- **Messaging:** “LUKU brings you buyers who are ready to buy – not just like and scroll.”
- **Comparison:** “Fewer DMs that go nowhere; more real carts and checkouts.”
- **Product:** Cart abandonment flows (e.g. reminders) and clear CTAs so “ready to buy” turns into orders.

---

## 7. New sellers are hard to discover without ads or influencers

**Their words:** *“New sellers are hard to discover without ads or influencers.”*

**LUKU angle:**
- **Merit-based discovery:** New arrivals, categories, style/color filters, and AI recommendations can surface new sellers without paid boost.
- **“New sellers” or “New this week”** section so new shops get a fair shot.

**Tactics to move sellers:**
- **Messaging:** “Get discovered without ad spend. New sellers get visibility; good listings get recommended.”
- **Product:** Dedicated “New sellers” / “New arrivals” and ensure recommendation logic doesn’t only favor big shops.
- **Seller story:** “I couldn’t afford IG ads; on LUKU I got my first sales from discovery, not from my following.”

---

## 8. Payments, deliveries, and follow-ups are manual and exhausting

**Their words:** *“Payments, deliveries, and follow-ups are manual and exhausting.”*

**LUKU angle:**
- **Payments:** Stripe in-app – no “send M-Pesa and screenshot.”
- **Orders:** One place for order list, status, and (where possible) tracking.
- **Follow-ups:** Order status updates and (future) in-app messaging or notifications reduce back-and-forth in DMs.

**Tactics to move sellers:**
- **Messaging:** “Get paid securely in-app. Manage orders and tracking in one place – no more spreadsheets or DM chaos.”
- **Seller dashboard:** Clear “Orders,” “To ship,” “Payments” so one glance replaces multiple chats.
- **Future:** Delivery partners or shipping options; optional in-app messages for order questions.
