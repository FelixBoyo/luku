## Promoting Your Personal Style Companion

**Last Updated:** January 2025  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active Strategy

---

## 🎯 Executive Summary

This marketing strategy document provides comprehensive guidance for promoting LUKU, an established AI-powered personal style companion platform. The strategy emphasizes our core differentiators—**DIY Mood Boards** and **AI-Driven Recommendations**—while maintaining alignment with our brand values of empowerment, sustainability, and inclusivity.

### Key Objectives
1. **Drive User Acquisition** - Attract new users through feature-focused campaigns
2. **Increase Engagement** - Encourage active use of mood boards and AI recommendations
3. **Build Brand Awareness** - Establish LUKU as the go-to personal style companion
4. **Highlight Innovation** - Showcase cutting-edge AI capabilities and user creativity

---

## 🌟 Core Features to Promote

### 1. DIY Mood Boards (Albums Feature)
**What it is:** Users can create custom collections of their favorite outfits, organizing them into beautiful, shareable mood boards.

**Key Benefits:**
- Express personal style through curated collections
- Organize outfits by occasion, season, or theme
- Visual inspiration at your fingertips
- Shareable style collections

**Marketing Angle:** "Your Style, Your Way - Create Beautiful Mood Boards That Tell Your Fashion Story"

### 2. AI-Driven Recommendations
**What it is:** Advanced AI that learns from user preferences, wardrobe items, and feedback to suggest personalized outfit combinations.

**Key Benefits:**
- Learns your style preferences over time
- Suggests outfits based on your existing wardrobe
- Adapts to your feedback (thumbs up/down)
- Saves time and reduces decision fatigue

**Marketing Angle:** "Your Personal Stylist That Never Sleeps - AI That Understands Your Unique Style"

### 3. Supporting Features to Highlight
- **Style Playground** - Experiment with style fusion and AI visualizations
- **Virtual Try-On** - See how outfits look before wearing them
- **Color Harmony** - Discover perfect color combinations
- **Trip Planner** - Smart packing with AI-generated outfit plans
- **Sustainability Tracking** - Make eco-conscious fashion choices

## 📱 Marketing Channels & Strategies

### Social Media Marketing

#### Platform-Specific Strategies

**Instagram**
- **Content Mix:**
  - 40% User-generated content (mood boards, outfit showcases)
  - 30% Feature tutorials (how to create mood boards, use AI recommendations)
  - 20% Style inspiration and trends
  - 10% Behind-the-scenes and brand personality

- **Post Types:**
  - Carousel posts: "5 Steps to Create Your First Mood Board"

## 💬 Messaging Framework

### Core Value Propositions

**For Mood Boards:**
- "Express your unique style through beautiful, curated collections"
- "Organize your favorite outfits and plan your looks"
- "Share your style journey and inspire others"

**For AI Recommendations:**
- "Your personal stylist that learns your style"
- "Discover new outfit combinations from your existing wardrobe"
- "Save time and reduce decision fatigue"

**For Platform Overall:**
- "Your Personal Style Companion"
- "Where Style Meets Sustainability"
- "Express yourself with confidence"

### Tone of Voice Guidelines

**Do:**
- ✅ Use empowering, positive language
- ✅ Celebrate individual expression
- ✅ Be conversational and friendly
- ✅ Focus on benefits, not features
- ✅ Use inclusive language

**Don't:**
- ❌ Use judgmental language
- ❌ Focus on "fixing" problems
- ❌ Be overly technical
- ❌ Make assumptions about users
- ❌ Use guilt-tripping tactics
