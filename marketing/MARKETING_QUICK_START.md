## ✍️ Content Creation Workflow

### For Instagram Posts
1. **Choose topic** from content calendar
2. **Write caption** following brand voice
3. **Create/select image** (use brand colors)
4. **Add hashtags** (5-10 relevant tags)
5. **Schedule post** for optimal time
6. **Prepare engagement** (respond to comments)

### For TikTok Videos
1. **Choose concept** (tutorial, tip, showcase)
2. **Film video** (15-60 seconds)
3. **Edit** (add captions, music, effects)
4. **Write description** with hashtags
5. **Post** at optimal time
6. **Engage** with comments

### For Blog Posts
1. **Choose topic** from content ideas
2. **Outline** key points
3. **Write** following brand guidelines
4. **Add images** and graphics
5. **Optimize** for SEO
6. **Publish** and share on social media

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Consistency is Key** - Post regularly to build audience
2. **Engage Authentically** - Respond to users genuinely
3. **Show, Don't Tell** - Use visuals to demonstrate features
4. **User-Generated Content** - Share user creations (with permission)
5. **Test and Learn** - Try different content types and analyze results
6. **Stay On Brand** - Always refer back to brand guidelines
7. **Be Responsive** - Quick responses build community
8. **Track Everything** - Data helps improve strategy

## 📞 Need Help?

### Common Questions

**Q: What if I don't know what to post?**  
A: Refer to the content calendar - it has daily themes and examples.

**Q: How do I handle negative feedback?**  
A: Respond professionally, acknowledge concerns, offer solutions, and escalate if needed.

**Q: Can I deviate from the content calendar?**  
A: Yes, but ensure content aligns with brand guidelines and monthly themes.

**Q: How often should I post?**  
A: Instagram: 5-7x/week, TikTok: 3-5x/week, Twitter: 3-5x/day, Pinterest: 10-20 pins/day

**Q: What if a feature isn't working?**  
A: Document the issue, inform the team, and create content around other features.
