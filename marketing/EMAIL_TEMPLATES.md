## ✅ Email Best Practices

### Subject Lines
- Keep under 50 characters
- Include emoji for visual appeal
- Create urgency when appropriate
- Personalize when possible
- A/B test different variations

### Preheaders
- Complement the subject line
- Provide additional context
- Keep under 90 characters
- Include a call-to-action hint

### Body Content
- Use clear, scannable formatting
- Include visual elements (images, buttons)
- Keep paragraphs short (2-3 sentences)
- Use bullet points for lists
- Include clear call-to-action buttons

### Timing
- **Welcome Series:** Send immediately after signup
- **Newsletters:** First Tuesday of each month
- **Campaigns:** Based on campaign schedule
- **Re-engagement:** 7, 14, 30 days after last activity

### Testing
- Test on multiple email clients
- Check mobile responsiveness
- Verify all links work
- Proofread for errors
- Test personalization variables
