## 📅 Recommended Workflow

### Daily (30-40 minutes)
1. Check `SOCIAL_MEDIA_CONTENT_CALENDAR.md` for today's theme
2. Create/post scheduled content
3. Engage with community (comments, DMs, shares)
4. Monitor analytics
5. Plan tomorrow's content

### Weekly (2-3 hours)
1. Review content calendar for the week
2. Create batch content (posts, videos, graphics)
3. Schedule social media posts
4. Send email campaigns (if scheduled)
5. Review weekly analytics
6. Adjust strategy based on performance

### Monthly (4-6 hours)
1. Update content calendar for next month
2. Review overall strategy performance
3. Plan monthly campaigns
4. Create monthly newsletter
5. Analyze metrics and KPIs
6. Update marketing documents as needed

## 🎨 Brand Compliance

Before publishing any content, ensure:
- ✅ "LUKU" is always uppercase
- ✅ Tagline included where appropriate: "Your Personal Style Companion"
- ✅ Colors match brand palette (Indigo to Purple gradient)
- ✅ Tone is empowering, inclusive, sustainable
- ✅ No judgmental or restrictive language
- ✅ Sustainability mentioned when relevant
- ✅ All links tested and working

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Consistency Wins** - Regular posting builds audience trust
2. **User-Generated Content** - Share user creations (with permission)
3. **Test and Learn** - Try different content types, analyze results
4. **Stay On Brand** - Always refer back to brand guidelines
5. **Engage Authentically** - Genuine responses build community
6. **Show, Don't Tell** - Visuals demonstrate features better than words
7. **Track Everything** - Data informs better decisions
8. **Be Responsive** - Quick responses show you care
