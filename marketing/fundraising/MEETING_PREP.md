## Anticipated questions & answers

### Market & problem

**Q: Is this a vitamin or a painkiller?**  
A: Daily painkiller for outfit decision fatigue (20–30 min/day). Weekly painkiller for sellers who can't convert social traffic. Commerce monetization is the painkiller for the business.

**Q: Why East Africa first?**  
A: Mobile-money maturity (M-Pesa), dense social-commerce seller base, urban youth fashion culture, and team market fluency. Novastar's core geography overlap.

**Q: How big can this get?**  
A: Start with EAC urban style-conscious consumers + social sellers (SOM). Expand to pan-African marketplace GMV. Long-term: style infrastructure layer (APIs, retail partnerships). Use researched SAM/SOM in deck.

### Product & technology

**Q: What's defensible?**  
A: Proprietary wardrobe graph + feedback loops. Match scores improve with wear logs, purchases, and thumbs up/down. Sellers matched to high-intent outfit-building moments — not generic browse.

**Q: AI costs?**  
A: Server-side Gemini via Express BFF; task-based routing (outfit suggestions, tagging). No client API keys. Costs scale with engagement but drive conversion — monitor cost per activated user vs. LTV.

**Q: Why mobile-native now?**  
A: Africa is mobile-first. Wardrobe upload (camera), push for OOTD, M-Pesa — native UX wins. Web remains reference; mobile is the product.

### Business model

**Q: How do you make money?**  
A: Marketplace take rate on GMV (primary). Creator affiliate on shoppable looks. Premium features later. Not dependent on ads day one.

**Q: What's your take rate?**  
A: `[X]%` — benchmarked against regional marketplaces; `[justify vs. value provided: trust, discovery, payments, AI matching]`.

**Q: Cold start — buyers or sellers first?**  
A: Parallel wedge: consumer utility (wardrobe + AI) works without marketplace; sellers need buyers. Launch with 20–50 anchor sellers + consumer activation funnel. Seller playbook targets IG pain (see `SELLER_ACQUISITION_BRAINSTORM.md`).

### Traction & metrics

**Q: What metrics matter now?**  
A: Activation: first wardrobe item, first mood board, first AI suggestion. Commerce: seller listing → first sale, repeat purchase rate. Retention: D7/D30 on outfit loop.

**Q: What do you have today?**  
A: `[Fill: web users, waitlist, beta testers, seller LOIs, GMV if any]`. Phase 2 mobile + BFF substantially built.

### Team

**Q: Why this team?**  
A: `[Founder market insight]` + `[technical execution on monorepo/BFF/mobile]` + `[growth/seller acquisition]`. Demonstrated discipline: phase gates, wave delivery, web-to-mobile pivot without rewrite.

**Q: What are you missing?**  
A: `[e.g. Head of ops/logistics, senior seller partnerships lead]` — hire post-seed with seller wave traction.

### Fundraise

**Q: How much and why?**  
A: **`[$X]`** for 18 months. 50% engineering (mobile v1 + checkout), 25% Kenya seller acquisition, 15% infra/AI, 10% buffer. Milestone: 50 sellers, paid orders, unit economics proof.

**Q: What happens if you miss milestones?**  
A: Wave 3 kill criteria: narrow to consumer-only utility or single-city geo before scaling CAC. Extend runway via scope reduction — already practiced in v1 cuts.

**Q: Exit?**  
A: Strategic (global fashion, telco, fintech, retail) · growth equity · long-term IPO at Africa scale GMV. 7–10 year build.

## Objection handling

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "Fashion is niche" | Daily frequency + commerce GMV + multi-sided platform. Not a styling toy — a transaction layer. |
| "Competing with Instagram/TikTok" | We don't compete on content. We capture **shop intent** when users build outfits. Sellers graduate from feed dependency. |
| "AI is a feature, not a company" | AI is the **retention engine**; marketplace is the **monetization engine**. Wardrobe data is the moat. |
| "Africa logistics are hard" | Terminal Africa T-Ship + local couriers (Ufanisi); phased Kenya-only fulfillment before cross-border. |
| "You'll burn on CAC" | Activation-led organic loops (mood boards, UGC) before paid scale. Falsifiable gates per wave. |

## Demo flow (15 minutes)

1. **Home — “Picked for You”** (3 min) — AI match %, hero product, commerce-first browse  
2. **Product detail** (2 min) — match score, seller trust, add to cart  
3. **Wardrobe** (2 min) — upload + tagging; “this powers your matches”  
4. **Checkout** (2 min) — M-Pesa / Paystack path (or mock if pre-integration)  
5. **Stylist** (3 min) — outfit from wardrobe → “complete the look” → shop gap  
6. **Seller angle** (2 min) — intent-qualified traffic vs. IG likes  
7. **Close** (1 min) — roadmap, ask, Q&A  

**Fallback:** If live demo fails, use screenshots + Loom recorded on device. Lead with Home commerce screenshots.
