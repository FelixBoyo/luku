## Mission (one sentence)

**LUKU helps everyday people shop smarter by matching them to fashion they will actually wear — using AI, their wardrobe, and their purchase history, with a trusted marketplace when they need something new.**

---

## Problem

Fashion-conscious consumers in Africa’s urban mass market buy impulsively from generic marketplaces and social feeds — purchases that don’t fit what they already own. Independent sellers on Instagram and WhatsApp struggle to convert views into sales and lack platform trust, secure checkout, and discovery beyond the feed algorithm. Daily outfit friction (20–30 min/day) compounds the gap between what people own and what they buy.

---

## Solution

**Primary — AI-matched commerce**

1. **Digitize the wardrobe** — AI image tagging; purchases sync back after checkout  
2. **Personalized marketplace** — products scored 0–100% against closet + purchase history  
3. **Buy with trust** — verified sellers, reviews, Stripe / Paystack / M-Pesa checkout  

**Secondary — Stylist & retention**

4. **AI outfit recommendations** — wear more of what you own (Google Gemini, server-side)  
5. **Mood boards** — plan looks; surface gaps to shop  
6. **Sustainability tracking** — wear logs, eco nudges, buy-smarter prompts  

**Differentiator:** The only shop that knows your closet — wardrobe-native AI commerce, not another generic feed or styling toy.
