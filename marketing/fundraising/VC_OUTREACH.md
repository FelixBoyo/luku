**To:** info@novastarventures.com · or partner intro via warm connection  
**Subject:** LUKU — wardrobe-aware fashion commerce for East Africa’s mass market

---

Dear Novastar Ventures team,

I am reaching out to introduce **LUKU**, a wardrobe-aware fashion commerce platform we are building for consumers and independent sellers across **East Africa** — starting in Kenya, Uganda, and Tanzania.

**The problem**  
Young urban Africans shop from generic marketplaces and social feeds — purchases that don't fit what they already own. Sellers on Instagram and WhatsApp get views but struggle to convert: no platform trust, manual payments, and discovery trapped behind the algorithm.

**Our solution**  
**LUKU is the only shop that knows your closet.** We use AI, wardrobe data, and purchase history to match users to products they'll actually wear (0–100% match scores), with verified checkout via **M-Pesa, Paystack, and Stripe**. An AI stylist and mood boards keep users engaged between purchases; commerce captures value when they're ready to buy. For sellers, we deliver intent-qualified buyers — not just scroll traffic.

**Why Novastar**  
Your thesis — backing commercial innovation that generates enduring value for **people and planet**, serving **mass-market latent demand** through tech-enabled models — maps directly to our approach:

- **Mass market, not luxury:** Free core features; revenue from everyday fashion transactions  
- **People in the value chain:** Independent sellers and creators earn through listings and “shop the look” affiliate flows  
- **Planet-positive:** Wear-more-from-wardrobe loops and conscious shopping — reducing waste without guilt-driven messaging  
- **Lean & fundamentals-first:** Phased mobile rollout, server-side AI cost control, and falsifiable wave gates before scaling acquisition — aligned with your guidance on unit economics and cash conservation  
- **Full-stack for broken chains:** We own wardrobe intelligence, trust, payments, and seller tooling — not a thin marketplace layer  

**Traction**  
We have a proven web-era commerce platform (AI-matched products, marketplace, seller dashboards) and are in **Phase 2**: native Expo mobile on an Express BFF. Home already surfaces “Picked for You” with AI match scores; wardrobe upload, cart, and M-Pesa checkout waves are in flight.

**The ask**
